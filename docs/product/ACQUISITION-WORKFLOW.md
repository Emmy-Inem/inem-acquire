# Acquisition workflow and launch capability contract

A small sales team should move from a target-customer profile to researched businesses, named contacts, outreach lists, qualified deals and dated follow-ups without duplicating work or guessing what a page means.

## Required outcomes

- Discovery targets 50 businesses per expanded search, deduplicates across sources and checks up to 50 websites with progress. Fewer available results must be explained; contacts and domains must never be invented to reach a quota.
- Catalog domains are historical candidates until checked. Blocked access is not evidence of a nonexistent domain. Replacement domains require matching company identity and corroborating public phone/location evidence. Keep review history and the old domain.
- Saved businesses retain structured phone/email details. Contacts are people or business inboxes attached to a business; lists group contacts for outreach; customer profiles describe who to target; deals represent qualified revenue opportunities; tasks represent a dated next action.
- Advisor answers the actual question using the selected organization's company context and sales evidence. Selected private documents can be included explicitly. A model outage leaves a truthful, question-focused evidence answer.
- Company identity supports a private logo/photo and private documents. Upload, open and delete have real permission checks, file validation and useful feedback.
- All data and evidence stay organization-scoped. AI sees only the authorized organization's context; website recovery receives public business details only. Suggested actions require user confirmation before writing.
- Tasks expose open, overdue, due-today and completed views, a meaningful empty state, completion/reopen, assignment and linked opportunities.

## Pricing and launch gates

Current published Starter/Growth prices remain unchanged. Research credits, team seats and offer/profile limits must be represented consistently between pricing and billing. A research credit means a business website research attempt, not browsing the shared catalog. Provider failures before research starts must not consume credits.

Paid campaign delivery and inbound email require a configured sending provider/domain. Paid subscription checkout requires live billing configuration. SSO, custom integrations and priority service are enterprise requirements to scope, not currently active self-service features. Do not claim these are operational or enable charging without end-to-end verification. Record the capability audit and remaining external gates with test evidence.

## Verification

Exercise creation, linking, editing, deletion, role denials, file upload/open/delete, company image isolation, task transitions, question-specific Advisor responses, 50-row research and website recovery failures. Render mobile and desktop states and run cross-organization tests. No anonymous access to private files or images. No guessed contact data or hidden aggregate database totals.
