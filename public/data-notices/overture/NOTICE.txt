Inem Acquire Discovery uses the Overture Maps Foundation Places release 2026-10-21.0.
https://overturemaps.org/
https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/#places

The catalog filters Nigerian records, normalizes contact formats and selects fields
for Discovery. Listings marked as needing review include uncategorized and broad
education listings. The original place ID and upstream source licenses are retained.

See the accompanying CDLA Permissive 2.0, CC0 1.0 and Apache 2.0 licenses.
The Foursquare Open Source Places data was transformed to the Overture schema and
further filtered and normalized by Inem Acquire. See NOTICE-Foursquare.txt.
